Our Impact

Environmental Sustainability

KTL is committed to sustainable manufacturing practices that protect our planet for future generations.

  • Water Conservation: Implemented closed-loop water systems reducing consumption by 40%
  • Energy Efficiency: Solar panels provide 30% of our facility's energy needs
  • Waste Reduction: 95% of production waste is recycled or repurposed
  • Eco-Friendly Materials: Increasing use of organic and recycled fibers

Social Impact

We believe in creating positive change in our communities and beyond.

  • Employment: 850+ direct jobs with fair wages and benefits
  • Education: Skills training programs for 200+ workers annually
  • Community Development: Supporting local schools and healthcare facilities
  • Women Empowerment: 60% of our workforce are women in leadership roles

Economic Impact

KTL contributes significantly to Bangladesh's export economy and local development.

  • Export Revenue: $15M+ annual export value to USA, Canada, and UK
  • Supply Chain: Supporting 50+ local suppliers and vendors
  • Innovation: R&D investment in sustainable textile technologies
  • Skills Development: Training programs for industry professionals

Certifications & Standards

Our commitment to excellence is recognized through international certifications:

  • ISO 9001:2015: Quality management system certification
  • C-TPAT: Customs-Trade Partnership Against Terrorism
  • WRAP: Worldwide Responsible Accredited Production
  • OEKO-TEX: Safe and sustainable textile certification

Future Goals

Our vision for continued positive impact:

  • Carbon neutral operations by 2030
  • 100% renewable energy by 2028
  • Zero waste to landfill by 2026
  • Expanding fair trade partnerships
